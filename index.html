The Crazy Old Drivers

Old rustbuckets, hairpin turns, and zero mercy between friends.

A small homemade retro racing game. Free, for Windows, macOS and Linux.

Content

Game modes

7 classes to vote on

Championship

3, 5 or 7 races of 3, 5 or 7 laps. Before each race, players vote for the vehicle class. The finale runs on a giant track, with a surprise challenge.

3 classes to vote on

Super Championship

Hypercar, Motorbike or Formula 1: 5 races on the class’s legendary circuits.

5 classes to vote on

Crazy Road

Roadster, GT, Hypercar, Motorbike or Formula 1: 3 or 5 stages of forks, roundabouts, chasms and spirals.

GT only

Rally

5 special stages on real mountain roads, split into sectors with changing weather.

Vehicle classes

Classic

Balanced. In the countryside.

Roadster

Nimble, slow on the straights. In the desert.

Monster truck

Tough, off-road. On construction sites, bumps and potholes included.

GT

Fast, heavy, slides around. In the middle of the city.

Hypercar

Fast, sturdy, sharp in corners. Endurance racing in the forest, in front of a huge crowd.

Motorbike

Explosive, agile, very fragile. On the hills, leaning into every corner.

Formula 1

Very fast, fragile. Between paddock and floodlights.

Weather, day and night

Day or night

Your pick, or drawn at random each race.

Surprise challenges

Thunderstorm, snow, fog, sandstorm, crosswind, earthquake or power outage.

Rain and fog

In the Super Championship and the Rally, the weather can turn mid-race.

Legendary circuits

Motorsport legends

Le Mans, Monaco, Mugello, Pikes Peak… and many more.

Plus 30+ homemade tracks

Jumps, tunnels, shortcuts, banked turns, and 5 giant tracks.

Crazy roads

It spins and it jumps

Roundabouts, spiral roads, loops and chasms to leap across.

And it never stops

Chicanes to weave through and trains: when the lights flash, better brake.

And also

Shop

Upgrade grip, turbo and top speed. Buy boosts: nitro, all-weather tyres, anchor, shield.

Hazards

Oil, puddles and tornado. Hit a wall too hard, and the helicopter has to drop in a brand new car.

Multiplayer

Up to 4 players online, on keyboard or gamepad.

Challenges and achievements

Plenty of goals: 50+ achievements to unlock, plus records and stats, solo and online.

News

v1.14.0 — November 20, 2026
  • New Time trial format: alone in Simulation, by day, with no weather, AI or pickups, on the super championships' real tracks. Tracks are picked from a list of cards (outline, name, record), and your best lap comes back as a ghost.
  • Time trial records join the Pantheon and are shared between friends in each lobby: the best time and who set it, then your own.
  • The ghost is more see-through.
  • Steering sensitivity: a new setting in the controls options (50% by default). Below 100%, a held key turns further and further instead of going to full lock at once, and a stick turns more gently. In a race, keypad 1 and 2 lower or raise it by 5%.
  • Loopings go 3D: cars climb in and go upside down at the top.
  • New achievements: perfect starts, countered attacks, time trial records beaten, and Purist for the 3 super championships won in Simulation.
  • Two new race tracks: Fast Forward Groove and Half-Time Stomp.
v1.13.0 — November 17, 2026
  • The scenery stands out: cars, buildings, trees, boats, animals and rocks now have volume.
  • Chasms have depth, and a car in mid-jump really rises above the track.
  • Wheels turn as the car drives.
  • Perfect start: hit the throttle just before the green light to launch with a burst of nitro. Too early and your wheels spin.
  • Suzuka: the ferris wheel can now be seen from the track.
  • Crossings: no more cutting through the other road.
  • Jumps in bends now land on the road.
  • Crazy Road: at a fork, the weather of the road not taken no longer shows.
  • Online: smoother play.
v1.12.1 — November 15, 2026
  • Final podium: the Achievement unlocked card no longer hides the list of unlocked achievements.
v1.12.0 — November 15, 2026
  • The game now speaks English: menus, races, shop, achievements, weather and vehicles translated, with an English announcer. New Language option in the General tab (defaults to the system language).
  • New Mode setting before the race: Arcade (as before), Sport (no keys, money or garage) and Simulation (Sport without oil, water or tornado, for super championships and rally). Each mode keeps its own records.
  • New Weather setting, on by default: turned off, no more weather events in championships and super championships.
  • Race settings no longer move around: the ones the format fixes stay visible, greyed out, showing their forced value.
  • The end of a race no longer turns black and white: the checkered flag, confetti and fireworks do the job.
  • The website is now in English by default (French page still available), with a new trailer in English and French.

Requirements

A desktop or laptop PC less than ten years old will do.

System
Windows 10 or newer, macOS, or Linux (64-bit)
Processor
64-bit Intel or AMD (x86_64). On Mac: Intel or Apple Silicon.
Disk space
About 120 MB
Graphics card
Vulkan-compatible: NVIDIA GTX 600, AMD Radeon HD 7000, Intel HD 500 or newer
Drivers
Up to date. On Linux, Vulkan drivers installed (Mesa or NVIDIA).
Controls
Keyboard or gamepad. Solo or online multiplayer.
